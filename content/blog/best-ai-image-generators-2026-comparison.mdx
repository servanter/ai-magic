## Prompt Engineering Tips by Platform

### Midjourney v7
**Best Practices:**
- Use specific lens and camera references (85mm, f/1.4)
- Include lighting details (golden hour, studio lighting)
- Specify aspect ratio with --ar flag
- Use --stylize for artistic control (0-1000)

**Example:** "Portrait of athlete, cinematic lighting, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, studio setting, --ar 2:3 --stylize 750"

### DALL·E 4
**Best Practices:**
- Front-load important elements (mention first)
- Separate text content with quotation marks
- Use comma-separated attribute lists
- Be explicit about spatial relationships

**Example:** "Coffee shop menu board, text says 'Espresso $4, Latte $5', chalk lettering on black board, wooden frame, soft window light"

### Adobe Firefly
**Best Practices:**
- Use simple, clear language (not technical)
- Specify commercial style categories
- Include brand safety keywords
- Leverage style presets before custom prompts

**Example:** "Professional team photo, modern office, diverse employees, corporate style, natural lighting, appropriate business casual attire"

### Stable Diffusion
**Best Practices:**
- Include negative prompts (what to avoid)
- Use keyword weighting with (keyword:1.2) syntax
- Specify sampling method and steps
- Reference trained model specializations

**Example:** "(masterpiece:1.3), anime character, (detailed eyes:1.2), blue hair, studio lighting | Negative: blurry, low quality, text, watermark"
