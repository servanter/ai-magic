## Privacy & Security Analysis

### How AI Email Tools Access Your Data

**Three models:**

1. **Metadata-only** (SaneBox, Clean Email):
   - Reads: sender, subject, date, your actions
   - **Doesn't read**: email body content
   - **Risk**: Low

2. **Full-access for processing** (Superhuman, Shortwave, Copilot):
   - Reads: full email content
   - **Usage**: Process locally or on their servers
   - **Storage**: Claims to not store content
   - **Risk**: Medium (trust required)

3. **Native platform AI** (Gmail, Outlook):
   - Reads: full email content
   - **Processing**: On Google/Microsoft servers
   - **Storage**: Already stored there
   - **Risk**: Low (if you trust Google/Microsoft)

### Privacy Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Avoid tools that:**
- Don't clearly state data handling practices
- Store email content indefinitely
- Share data with third parties for ads
- Headquartered in countries with weak privacy laws
- Can't delete your data upon request

### Security Best Practices

✅ **Always:**
- Use OAuth (not IMAP passwords)
- Enable 2FA on email accounts
- Review permissions granted (revoke unused apps)
- Check privacy policy annually

✅ **For sensitive emails:**
- Use encrypted email (ProtonMail, Tuta)
- Exclude work accounts from third-party AI tools
- Check employer IT policy first

### Trust Rankings (Privacy + Security)

1. **Most trusted**: Gmail AI, Copilot (native platforms)
2. **High trust**: SaneBox, Clean Email (metadata-only)
3. **Medium trust**: Superhuman, Shortwave (reputable, VC-backed)
4. **Low trust**: Unknown startups with vague policies

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Signing Up for Too Many Tools

**The trap:** "I'll use SaneBox for triage, Flowrite for drafting, Boomerang for scheduling..."

**Result:** Tool overload, conflicting rules, confusion.

**Fix:** Pick **one primary tool** (Superhuman, Shortwave, or SaneBox), use native features for the rest.

---

### Mistake 2: Not Training the AI

**The trap:** Install SaneBox, expect perfect triage day 1.

**Reality:** AI learns from your behavior. Takes 1-2 weeks.

**Fix:**
- **Week 1**: Actively move emails to correct folders
- **Week 2**: AI starts getting better
- **Week 3+**: Mostly hands-off

---

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Privacy Settings

**The trap:** Click "allow all permissions" without reading.

**Risk:** Grant unnecessary access (send emails, delete emails, access contacts).

**Fix:** Review OAuth permissions. Revoke unused permissions in account settings.

---

### Mistake 4: Over-Automating

**The trap:** Auto-archive everything from certain senders.

**Risk:** Miss important emails (e.g., boss sends personal check-in).

**Fix:** Review auto-rules weekly. Keep human oversight for VIPs.

---

### Mistake 5: Not Unsubscribing Aggressively

**The trap:** Let AI filter newsletters to @SaneNews folder.

**Better:** **Unsubscribe** if you haven't read in 3 months.

**Result:** Reduce inbox by 40-60%, make AI more accurate.

**Tools:** SaneBlackHole, Clean Email Unsubscriber, Gmail's "Unsubscribe" link.

### Q: Are AI email tools safe?

**A:** Mostly yes, with caveats:
- **Native platforms** (Gmail, Outlook) are safest (data already there)
- **Metadata-only tools** (SaneBox) are very safe
- **Full-access tools** (Superhuman) require trust (read their privacy policy)
- **Red flag**: Avoid unknown startups with vague policies

**Action:** Review OAuth permissions, enable 2FA, check privacy policies.

---

### Q: Will AI replace my email assistant?

**A:** Not yet. AI tools handle:
- ✅ Triage (what's important?)
- ✅ Drafting simple replies ("Thanks!", "Got it")
- ✅ Scheduling meetings (coordinate calendars)

But struggle with:
- ❌ Complex negotiations
- ❌ Sensitive communications (HR, legal)
- ❌ Context-heavy decisions

**Timeline:** By 2028, AI might handle 70-80% of email tasks (up from 40% today).

---

### Q: How long until I see results?

**A:** Depends on tool:
- **Gmail AI**: Immediate (Smart Reply works day 1)
- **SaneBox**: 1-2 weeks (learning period)
- **Superhuman**: 2-3 weeks (learning keyboard shortcuts)
- **Clean Email**: Immediate (bulk cleanup)

**Tip:** Stick with it for 1 month before judging.

---

### Q: Can I use multiple AI email tools together?

**A:** Yes, but be careful:
- **Good combo**: SaneBox (triage) + Flowrite (drafting)
- **Bad combo**: SaneBox + Superhuman (conflicting triage)

**Rule:** One tool per function (triage, drafting, scheduling).

---

### Q: What if I have multiple email accounts?

**A:** Check multi-account support:
- **Works**: SaneBox, Clean Email, Gmail (switch accounts)
- **Doesn't work**: Superhuman (1 account/subscription), Shortwave (1 account)

**Cost:** Some tools charge per account (Clean Email: $20 for 3 accounts).

---

### Q: Do AI email tools work offline?

**A:** Mostly no:
- **Requires internet**: All AI processing (cloud-based)
- **Exception**: Apple Intelligence (on-device processing, works offline)

**Workaround:** Native email clients (Apple Mail, Outlook) cache emails for offline reading.

---

### Q: How much training data do they need?

**A:** Varies:
- **SaneBox**: 50-100 emails (1-2 weeks)
- **Gmail AI**: 1,000+ emails (learns over months)
- **Superhuman**: Immediate (but you need to learn keyboard shortcuts)

**Tip:** The more you interact (move emails, mark important), the faster it learns.

---

### Q: Can I cancel anytime?

**A:** Yes, all tools offer:
- Monthly subscriptions (cancel anytime)
- 30-day free trials (except Gmail, which is always free)
- Pro-rated refunds (some)

**Note:** Annual plans save 20-30% but lock you in.

---

### Q: What happens to my data if I cancel?

**A:** Depends:
- **Metadata-only** (SaneBox): Nothing happens (they delete data)
- **Full-access** (Superhuman): You lose AI features, keep emails
- **Native** (Gmail): Nothing happens (data stays in Gmail)

**Action:** Export important rules/settings before canceling.

### Superhuman Power User Guide

**Keyboard shortcuts that changed my life:**

- **Cmd+K**: Search everything (emails, contacts, files)
- **E**: Archive (done with email)
- **H**: Snooze (defer until later)
- **Cmd+Shift+L**: Add reminder ("Reply by Friday")
- **Cmd+Enter**: Send email
- **Cmd+Shift+M**: Move to folder

**Speed techniques:**
1. **Triage mode**: Swipe through inbox in 10 minutes (vs. 30 minutes)
2. **Snippets**: Save common replies (e.g., "meeting-confirm" expands to full text)
3. **Split Inbox**: Process VIPs first, newsletters last
4. **Read Receipts**: See who opened emails (useful for sales)

**Mobile workflow:**
- Desktop: Deep work, drafting, triage
- Mobile: Quick replies, checking urgent emails

**Cost justification:**
- **Break-even**: Save 25 min/day at $72/hour = $450/month value
- **Reality**: Most save 20 min/day = $360/month value
- **Verdict**: Worth it if you make over $50/hour + process 100+ emails/day

**Best for:** Founders, executives, sales teams, VCs.

### Gmail AI Hidden Features

**Underused features:**

1. **Smart Compose personalization**: Settings → Enable personalization (learns your writing style)
2. **High-priority notifications**: Only get notified for important emails
3. **Nudges**: Reminds you to follow up (disable if annoying)
4. **Unsubscribe suggestions**: Gmail suggests newsletters to unsubscribe

**Power tip:** Combine with filters:

**Limitations:**
- Smart Compose sometimes awkward ("Thanks for that thing...")
- Priority Inbox learns slowly (takes 3+ months)

**Best for:** Gmail users under 50 emails/day.
